About This Research Platform

This platform leverages state-of-the-art deep learning models to assist medical professionals in detecting COVID-19 from chest X-ray images.

  • U-Net Advanced image segmentation architecture for precise lung area isolation and feature extraction.
  • VGG-19 Deep convolutional neural network for multi-class classification (Normal, Pneumonia, COVID-19).
Research Note: This tool is designed for research purposes and should be used alongside professional medical diagnosis.
